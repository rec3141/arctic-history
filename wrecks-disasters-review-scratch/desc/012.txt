Gazetteer entry. The single sheet found at Victory Point says the ships were deserted on 22 April 1848, five leagues north-north-west of that spot, after being beset since 12 September 1846; that Sir John Franklin died on 11 June 1847; and that 105 survivors under Francis Crozier and James Fitzjames were starting next day for the Back River. None reached it. Both hulls were found by following Inuit testimony: HMS Erebus in Wilmot and Crampton Bay on 2 September 2014, in the water Inuit accounts had pointed to for a century and a half and that Louie Kamookak of Uqsuqtuuq spent his life collecting; and HMS Terror in Terror Bay on 3 September 2016, after Sammy Kogvik of Uqsuqtuuq told the searchers about a mast he had seen standing out of the ice there.
