A Parks Canada team reached Banks Island on 22 July 2010 and began a side-scan sonar survey of Mercy Bay three days later. HMS Investigator appeared on the record about a quarter of an hour after the first line was run, sitting upright in roughly eight metres of water with her upper works gone but her hull standing. She had been afloat, then driven aground, then crushed and sunk by the ice of the bay some time after Robert McClure's crew left her in 1853. The wreck lies just outside Aulavik National Park; McClure's Cache and the three graves are inside it, and the site is managed together with the Inuvialuit whose ancestors salvaged the cache for generations. The point plotted here is Mercy Bay itself: Parks Canada has not published a surveyed position for the hull, and the site is protected.
