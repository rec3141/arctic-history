Nine hundred and fifty-eight pages of despatches, journals and courts martial laid before Parliament in January 1855, and the official record behind most of the 1853-54 entries in this gazetteer. It carries Commander William Pullen's North Star journal at Beechey Island, which records the loss of the Breadalbane by signal from the Phoenix on 21 August 1853 with the note "no loss of life", and the return of Johnson and Hook, the two survivors of Bellot's party, on the same day; Edward Inglefield's proceedings in the Phoenix; Belcher's and Kellett's despatches on the abandonment of the four ships, including the order Kellett headed with his beset position in Barrow Strait; Robert McClure's notice left in the cairn at the Bay of Mercy and his letter reporting that the Investigator was left on the evening of 3 June 1853 with her colours flying and her hatches battened; and Pullen's account of the squadron's departure on 27 August 1854.
