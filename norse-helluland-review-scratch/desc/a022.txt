The largest and most lavish of Icelandic medieval codices, written in the 1380s, and the sole witness to Groenlendinga saga - the saga that gives the first sighting of the western lands to Bjarni Herjolfsson and the naming of Helluland to Leif Eiriksson. If this one manuscript had burned, half the evidence for the Norse voyages to North America would not exist and the surviving half would tell a different story. Reeves prints the Flatey Book Wineland history in full with phototype plates of the vellum. The book was taken to Denmark in 1656 and returned to Iceland in 1971.
