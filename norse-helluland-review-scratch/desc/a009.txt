A SAGA ROUTE, NOT A LOG. Every waypoint but the first is inferred. Groenlendinga saga gives no coordinates, no dates within the voyage and no distances except sailing days; the only firm geography is the order of the three lands and the character of each coast. Helluland is placed here on the east coast of Qikiqtaaluk (Baffin Island), the usual modern identification, but Gathorne-Hardy himself put Helluland in Newfoundland and Labrador taken as one country, or perhaps Newfoundland alone, and W. Hovgaard in 1915 put it at Resolution Island in the mouth of Hudson Strait. Read the line as a diagram of the saga's claims, not as a track.
