William Baffin, 4 July 1616, at the top of the bay north-west of Kalaallit Nunaat: "In this Sound we saw great numbers of whales, therefore we called it Whale Sound, and doubtlesse, if we had beene prouided for killing of them, we might haue strooke very many. It lyeth in the latitude 77° 30′." This is the highest latitude Baffin states for a place he actually entered. Two centuries later the same information brought the British whaling fleets to the head of Baffin Bay.
