Gloucester; cape, Boothia pena., Frank.; named by Boss, 1831, after H.R.H. the Princess Mary (1776-1857), fourth daughter of George III. Or, after her husband, and first cousin, H.R.H. William Frederick, Duke of Gloucester and Edinburgh. She was the last surviving of the 15 children of George III.
Gods Mercy; bay, Southampton I., Kee. ; named by Capt. Lyon, 1824, as he and his companions were delivered from shipwreck in a storm while here.
Gods Mercy; bay, Southampton I., Kee.; named by Lyon, 1824; he named them the isles of God’s mercies’ in gratitude for his escape from shipwreck; he sailed over a rock which at low tide, stood two fathoms and a half above water.
