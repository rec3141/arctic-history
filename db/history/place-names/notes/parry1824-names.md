### Ambiguities and OCR Issues

1. **Line 8724**: "Culgrufp Creek" — likely OCR error for "Culgrave Creek" or similar
2. **Line 17330**: "Neuyumg-Et-dun" — Winter Island Inuit name with OCR inconsistencies
3. **Line 26304**: Amherst Island — text indicates this may be one of two islands delineated by Iligliuk in her charts
4. **Line 26048**: "Cape Ossory" — commemorative subject unclear; possibly refers to "Ossory" (historical Irish kingdom/region)
5. **Line 7041**: "Georgina Island" — named after Georgina (female name, but specific person not identified in text)

### Research Notes

- Many place names honor Admiralty patrons and Navy personnel in hierarchical order
- Captain Lyon co-names several features with Parry (Barrow River, Duckett's Cove)
- Iligliuk, the principal Inuit female informant, played a crucial role in mapping and provided native names for several places
- The "Hurd Channel" (Hurd Passage) was named for the Hydrographer to the Admiralty, a position of high importance
- Some places were named at the specific "desire" or "request" of subordinates (Hooper, Bushnan, Sherer, Ross, Hoppner, Fisher), suggesting collaborative naming practices
- Five places are named after close personal friends, using language like "my friend" or "late companion"
