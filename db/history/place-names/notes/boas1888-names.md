## Key Geographic Terms from Boas

**Regional Divisions:**
- **Oqo** (weather side/lee side) = Cumberland Sound and southeastern Davis Strait
- **Aggo** (weather side/northwest) = Prince Regent Inlet and northern Baffin
- **Akudnirn** (intervening country) = Central Davis Strait between Aggo and Oqo

**Cumberland Sound Subdivisions:**
- **Talirpia** (right side/southwest) = West shore
- **Saumia** (left side/northeast) = East shore and southeastern highlands
- **Qingua** (head) = Head of the sound, upper estuary
- **Kingnait** (high land) = Highland dividing Saumia and Qingua

**Notes:**
- Page references are approximate based on OCR line numbers
- Qeqerten = Kekerten (whaling station, now in Nunavut)
- Pangnirtung valley connects Cumberland Sound to Davis Strait
- Nettilling = major lake north of Cumberland Sound (now Nettilling Lake)
- Home Bay is visited by northern tribes for summer hunting
