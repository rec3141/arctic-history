**Key backers (Company of Merchants Discoverers of the North-West Passage):**
- Sir Thomas Smith — Merchant, Governor of East India Company, wealthy London merchant
- Sir Dudley Digges — Diplomat, merchant, scholar
- Sir John Wolstenholme — Merchant, prominent in merchant companies
- Sir James Lancaster — Merchant-knight, East India Company official
- Sir Francis Jones — Alderman, merchant
- Alderman William Cockayne — Governor of Eastland Company

**Geography:** Lancaster Sound and Jones Sound mark the boundary between North (High Arctic inlets, Baffin Bay proper) and South (Hudson Strait, Davis Strait, Ungava Bay, Greenland coast).

**Unclear names:** Hakluyt Island (likely Richard Hakluyt but not explicitly named in text); Worsenhams Cape attribution uncertain; Mount Charles and Deeps Cape may reference officials or features rather than individual patrons.
