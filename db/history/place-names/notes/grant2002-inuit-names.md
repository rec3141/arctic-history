# Inuit Place Names from Shelagh Grant, Arctic Justice (2002)

## Appendix 2: Inuit Place Names for North Baffin Island

Extracted from Appendix 2 (pages 265-275) of *Arctic Justice: On Trial for Murder, Pond Inlet, 1923*

| Inuit Name | English Name | Meaning | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggu (Agu) | winter camp north of Fury and Hecla Strait | "an Inuit winter camp on the Baffin coast" | 272 |
| Aivilik | area around Repulse Bay | "name once used to describe the general area" | 272 |
| Aminaarjuaq (Amadjuak) | abandoned HBC post | "now abandoned, the site of a Hudson's Bay Company post in the early 1920s on the southern coast of Baffin Island" | 272 |
| Aukarniruaq | Iglullingmiut winter camp | "where the sea never freezes"; an Iglullingmiut winter camp on the Baffin coast near Fury and Hecla Strait | 273 |
| Igarjuaq | Albert Harbour, Eclipse Sound | "the big fireplace" (resembled a qullik or stone lamp) | 273 |
| Igloolik (Igloolic) | Igloolik Island | "the place where there are igloos or houses" | 273 |
| Igluligarjuaq (Chesterfield Inlet) | Chesterfield Inlet | "a place with few houses" | 273 |
| Ikpiarjuk (Arctic Bay) | Arctic Bay, Admiralty Inlet | "a pocket" (surrounded by mountains on three sides) | 273 |
| Iqaluit (in Tay Sound) | Tay Sound | "the place where there are Arctic char" | 273 |
| Iqalungnit | hunting camp site | "site of the hunting camp where Nuqallaq died in late November 1925" | 273 |
| Kangiq (Cape Crauford) | Cape Crauford, Brodeur Peninsula | "a high point of land or promontory" | 273 |
| Kangigtuasluk (Cumberland Sound) | Cumberland Sound | "this large body of water is surrounded by high mountains" | 273 |
| Kangigtuqaapik (Clyde River) | Clyde River, south of Pond Inlet | "nice little inlet near a high point of land" | 273 |
| Kimmiruit (Lake Harbour) | Lake Harbour | "heel," referring to shape of rocky outcrop protruding into bay | 274 |
| Kinngait (Cape Dorset) | Cape Dorset | "high mountains" | 274 |

| Nallua / Nadiua (near Low Point) | Bylot Island | "where the caribou crossed" to Bylot Island | 274 |
| Naujaat (Repulse Bay) | Repulse Bay | "small gulls" in reference to seagull nesting place | 274 |
| Panniqtuuq (Pangnirtung) | Pangnirtung, Cumberland Sound | "the place of the bull caribou" | 274 |

| Qilalukkat (Salmon River) | Salmon River, Eclipse Sound | running into Eclipse Sound west of Pond Inlet | 274 |

| Sirmik | Admiralty Inlet islands | "group of islands near the head of Admiralty Inlet" | 275 |

| Tikkakal (Tokeekaan) | near Cape Henry Kaler | trading post location | 275 |
| Titiralík (Canada Point) | Canada Point, Navy Board Inlet | "where there is writing" (Bernier's ship name and date carved in rock) | 275 |
| Tuujjuk (Adams Island) | Adams Island, Lancaster Sound | entrance to Navy Board Inlet | 275 |
| Tulukkaat | Patricia River mouth, Eclipse Sound | "the ravens" | 275 |
| Turluq (Tooloorag/Low Point) | Navy Board Inlet | point of land mid-way along west side | 275 |
| Tunuriaq | Eclipse Sound area | "in the shade of the big mountain" (Tuniq mountain range) | 275 |
| Tununirusiq | Arctic Bay area | "in the shade of the lesser mountains" | 275 |
| Usualuk (Osho-Afio Harbour) | Cumberland Sound | "the place where there are clams" | 275 |

## Additional Place Name References Found in Chapters

- **Sanniruit (Button Point)**: Mentioned on page 135-140 as location of Arctic Gold Exploration Syndicate trading post and AGES post
- **Eclipse Sound**: Referenced on page 136, 138, 139 as major geographic feature
- **Albert Harbour**: Mentioned on page 138 as location of HBC supplies unloading
- **Salmon River**: Discussed on page 139 as selected site for RCMP post (by Joy)
