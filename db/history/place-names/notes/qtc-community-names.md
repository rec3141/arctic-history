Extracted from Qikiqtani Truth Commission Community Histories 1950–1975

## Pond Inlet (Mittimatalik)

**Inuktitut Name:** Mittimatalik (p. 132)

**Meaning:** "the people of the shaded place" or Mittimatalingmiut, meaning "people of Mittimatalik" (p. 138)

**English Name:** Pond Inlet / Pond's Bay

**English Name Origin:** p. 142: "The place name 'Pond's Bay' was chosen by an explorer in 1818 in honour of an English astronomer."

## Kinngait (Cape Dorset)

**Inuktitut Name:** Kinngait (p. 157)

**Meaning:** p. 157: "Inuit named the area Kinngait, which describes the high, undulating hills surrounding the community's small, protected harbour."

**Alternative Names:** Also known as Sikusilaq, with people called Sikusilarmiut, "a reference to the place of open water in winter" (p. 160-161)

**English Name:** Cape Dorset (formerly)

**English Name Origin:** p. 438-440: Named by Captain Luke Foxe in 1631 "for the fourth earl of Dorset, an English courtier, diplomat, and promoter of colonization."

## Arctic Bay (Ikpiarjuk)

**Inuktitut Name:** Ikpiarjuki (p. 153)

**Meaning:** p. 153-154: "The Inuktitut name means 'pocket', which refers to the way the hamlet is nestled between tall hills."

**Regional Name:** Tununirusiq, "which refers to the big mountain in front of Arctic Bay that faces south" (p. 158)

**English Name:** Arctic Bay

**English Name Origin:** p. 291-293: "Its current name references the whaling vessel Arctic that surveyed the area in 1872 under the command of Captain William Adams."

**Other Place Names Glossed:**
- Adams Sound (p. 156)
