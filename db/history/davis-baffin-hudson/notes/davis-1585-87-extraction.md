## Corrections checked against the OCR by run 3, 2026-09-08

All three Davis voyages are now in the database as tracks 001, 002 and 003 with
spans, events and quotes. The following points in the table above are wrong or
incomplete; the database rows are correct.

- **1585 return is 30 September**, not 29. The narrative reads "The 30 of
  September wee came into Dartmouth" (p. 14). Markham's introduction says the
  30th too (p. xii); the "29" in the earlier note came from a different page.
- **1587 return is 15 September.** Davis's letter to Sanderson is dated
  Sandridge, 16 September 1587 and opens "Yesterday, the 15 of September, I
  landed all wearie" (p. 60). No `after`/season qualifier is needed.
- **The 1586 kidnapping is 11 July**, at Gilbert Sound (pp. 25-26). A man was
  taken as a hostage for a stolen anchor; the anchor was returned, and Davis
  sailed with him anyway when the wind came fair. The printed margin reads
  "One of the people taken, which after dyed". His name is not recorded.
- **There is a second kidnapping, 19 June 1587** (p. 41): "the master of the
  Sunneshine tooke one of the people, a very strong lustie yong fellow."
  Markham's introduction (p. xxviii) adds "What became of him is not related."
- **"Furious Overfall" is not Davis's phrase.** It is the legend on the
  Molyneux globe of 1592. Davis's own words are "we passed by a very great
  gulfe, the water whirling and roring, as it were the meeting of tides"
  (p. 47, 31 July 1587), and the day before, off Lumley's Inlet, "the water, as
  it were, whirling and overfalling, as if it were the fall of some great water
  through a bridge" (p. 46). Markham makes the distinction at p. xxxvi.
- **1586 violence.** Two of Davis's sailors were killed and two wounded by
  arrows on the Labrador coast on 6 September (p. 30). Separately, the
  Sunneshine's own narrative records a fight at Gilbert Sound on 30 August in
  which her crew killed three Kalaallit (p. 37), and the loss of the pinnace
  Northstarre with all hands on 3 September (p. 38).
- **The Mermayde was not sent home from Sukkertoppen in July.** Davis left her
  at anchor and went on in the Moonelight on 12 August 1586 (p. 27).
- Davis's 1586 vocabulary of about forty Kalaallit words is at pp. 21-22, with
  Hinrich Rink's identifications in a footnote. Not yet an artifact; it should be.
