# Simmons's botany on the Second Fram Expedition (Sverdrup, New Land, vol. 2)
Extracted from db/history/sverdrup-bernier-peary/files/sverdrup-bernier-peary-sverdrup-newland-v[12].txt

Appendix: "Summary of the botanical work of the expedition, and its results", by Herman G. Simmons.

## Dated flowering and collecting
- 1898-08-21 Cape Rutherford: Papaver radicatum "still in full bloom"; nearly all other flowers over except Saxifraga tricuspidata.
- 1899-06-01 Hayes Sound, first botanical excursion of the year: "Saxifraga oppositifolia, ever the most forward, was here and there with partially or entirely open flowers."
- 1899-08-26 Framfjord (south coast of Ellesmere, behind Cone and Smith Islands): first visit.
- 1900-05 (early) Havnefjord: "Salix arctica began to show bursting buds."
- 1900-06-06 Havnefjord: Saxifraga oppositifolia in flower.
- 1900-06-11 Havnefjord: Salix arctica in flower.
- 1900-06-30 Havnefjord: nineteen flowering species noted before the end of the month.
- 1900-08-11 North Devon peninsula: nineteen phanerogams observed.
- 1901-06-15 west coast (Axel Heiberg): Schei saw Saxifraga oppositifolia in bloom.
- 1901-06-18 Gaasefjord: first day with a mean temperature above 0 C.
- 1901-06-22 Gaasefjord: Saxifraga oppositifolia found in blossom near winter quarters.
- 1901-06-30 Gaasefjord: only Draba hirta besides came into flower before the end of the month.
- 1902-06-07 Baumann Fiord: Saxifraga oppositifolia showed its first blooms.
- 1902-06-30: twenty-five species seen in flower before the end of the month, more than in either preceding year.
- 1901-07 Ytre Eide bird-rock: Bryum capillare a foot deep.

## Totals
- Foulke Fiord: about seventy species known after Simmons added thirty-three to Hart's list.
- Ellesmere Land: about one hundred vascular plants; with the lower plants "probably the aggregate number of species will at least reach four hundred".
- Specimens catalogued: 1,078 phanerogam numbers, 4,064 numbers in all; about five hundred moss numbers.
- Determinations divided: mosses by Bryhn, calcareous algae by Foslie, lichens by Darbishire, fungi by Rostrup.
- Nomenclature after Lange's Conspectus Florae groenlandicae.
- Separate report: the vascular plants of Ellesmereland, first published in Nyt Magazin for Naturvidenskaberne.

## New to Ellesmere Land (Framfjord and Havnefjord, 1899-1902)
Pedicularis lanata, Armeria sibirica, Saxifraga Hirculus, Potentilla Vahliana, Eutrema Edwardsii,
Braya purpurascens, Pleuropogon Sabinei, Trisetum subspicatum, Elyna spicata, Chrysosplenium
tetrandrum, Saxifraga aizoides, Carex capillaris, C. ustulata, C. rupestris, Kobresia caricina,
Dupontia Fisheri.

## Use
- Bryum capillare and Sphagnum: "used by the Eskimo as wicks for their train-oil lamps" (Simmons).
- Cladonia rangiferina named as reindeer-moss on the west coast.
