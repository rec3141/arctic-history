flower several days later.

Other dated notes:
- 2 June 1962, Ruggles River: Saxifraga oppositifolia in flower, found by G. Hattersley-Smith.
- 16 June 1962: the 1961 seeds of Dryas integrifolia just starting to shed.
- about 5 August 1962: the 1962 Dryas seeds reached the same stage.
- 7 August 1962: seed shedding of Dryas, Eriophorum scheuchzeri and E. triste more advanced
  than the 1961 seed had been on 16 June.
- 29 June 1962: Oxyria digyna nearly in full fruit on a south slope at 760 m.
- 14 July 1962: Salix arctica at 600-700 m completely mature, capsules splitting, foliage yellow.
- Total flora of the Lake Hazen basin estimated at about 115 species.
- Powell (1961) recorded 35 vascular species at or above 1060 m on nunataks at the Gilman Glacier.
- Cover: 1 to 5 per cent on clay plains; over 75 per cent on the muddy delta.
- Bryophytes identified by W. C. Steere of the New York Botanical Garden.
- Pedicularis sudetica found at pond 17 by B. Hocking in 1963.
- Cochlearia officinalis: a single plant on the wet silt of the Blister Creek delta.
