# C.G.S. Arctic, 1906-07 (Bernier) — chronological positions

Source: `sverdrup-bernier-peary-bernier-report-1909.txt` — OCR of Captain J. E. Bernier's
official report on the 1906-07 voyage of the C.G.S. *Arctic* (Ottawa, Government Printing
Bureau, 1909). Page numbers below are the printed report's own pagination where legible in
the OCR running text (chapter breaks below drawn from the Contents page: Ch. I p.1, Ch. II
p.12, Ch. III p.20, Ch. IV p.27, Ch. V p.39, Ch. VI p.47, Ch. VII p.53, Ch. VIII p.69,
Ch. IX p.71). Where no printed page number could be read with confidence, "?" is used
rather than guessed.

Longitude is given WEST NEGATIVE (decimal degrees). Latitude is decimal degrees, N positive.

Rows are in voyage order.

## Notes on OCR quality

The narrative prose (day-by-day log, possession documents) is generally clean and reliable.
The worst OCR is in: (1) tabular material — the whale-catch statistics (pp. 71-74) and the
Port Burwell temperature table (Appendix, ~p. 79ff.) are too garbled by column misalignment
to extract reliably and are not reproduced here; (2) a handful of running heads/page numbers
in image-caption blocks, silently skipped; (3) isolated digit/letter swaps in coordinates,
flagged inline above where caught (Bylot Island longitude 60° vs 80°50'W; Melville I.
longitude 100°02' vs 106°02'W; "77° 07' E." for what should almost certainly read N.).
