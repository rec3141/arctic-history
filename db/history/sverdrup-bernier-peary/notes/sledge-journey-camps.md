# Dated camps of the second Fram expedition's sledge journeys

Extracted from Sverdrup, New Land (1904), vols I and II, and entered as the
waypoints of artifacts sverdrup-bernier-peary-300 to -304. Coordinates are
reconstructions except where the narrative gives an observation (marked in the
note). Place-name positions are from the Canadian Geographical Names Database.
