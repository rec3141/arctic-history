books, Settle, Ellis, Lok's papers and the State Papers on the ore, then an
appendix printing Hall's 1863 catalogue of the Frobisher relics.

## frobisher-hall-1862-rgs-frobisher-strait.txt
The whole file is the RGS meeting of 13 April 1863: Hall's paper at pp. 99-100
and the discussion (Back, Sherard Osborn on William Penny and "my knowledge is
money", Latrobe, Rae on Inuit truthfulness) at pp. 100-102.

## frobisher-cheshire1980-eskimos-in-england.pdf
Archivaria 10, pp. 23-50. The Bristol arrival and the Avon display are at
pp. 30-31; the White drawings at pp. 33-35; the deaths, the St Stephen's
register entry and the child's burial at pp. 37-39; Dodding's post-mortem in
translation at pp. 40-42; the medical commentary, including the identification
of the fall with Conyer's throw, at pp. 42-45.

## Not read

inventory papers; the whole of Best's book I (the geographical argument that
all parts of the world are habitable).
