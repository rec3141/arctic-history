# Plate and table index — posts-rcmp-missions

Method: `pdfinfo`, `pdfimages -list`, `pdftotext -layout` split on form-feed, scripted word-count
and caption-keyword sweep (Fig./Plate/Table/Map/Frontispiece), cross-checked against each book's
own printed "List of Illustrations" / "List of Tables" / "List of Figures" front matter where
present, and against printed page numbers recovered from the OCR text (running heads/folios).
No page images were viewed — everything below is inferred from OCR text and image-object counts.
Verify visually before cutting.

*count* does not distinguish plate pages from text pages. Plate pages were instead identified by

## B. Usher, *Fur Trade Posts of the Northwest Territories 1870–1970* (1971)

File: `posts-rcmp-missions/files/posts-rcmp-missions-usher1971-furtradeposts.pdf`
92 pages (pdfinfo). Page-scan PDF (LuraTech/Internet Archive-style derivative): every page carries
exactly 3 image objects (main + alt + soft mask), one full-page scan per page — image count does
not distinguish content. Book supplies its own "Table of Contents," "List of Tables," and "List
of Figures" (pdf pages 7–8), which give authoritative captions and printed page numbers for all
11 figures and 10 numbered tables; these were cross-mapped to pdf page indices using the printed
folios recovered by OCR (confirmed: each pdf page after the front matter reproduces a two-page
book spread, so printed page = roughly 2×pdf_page − 4 / − 3). Direct caption text was found in
the OCR for the great majority of entries, confirming the mapping; a few (marked "position by
front-matter listing") could not be read directly because they are graphic-only maps/charts with
no OCR-able caption text on the page, but their position is well constrained by the two
neighbouring printed folios.

This is fundamentally a gazetteer report: alongside the 10 numbered tables, Part Two (pdf pages
77–92) is one continuous run of "Post Code / Location / Dates of Operation / Status" tables,
organized by owner (Hudson's Bay Company, Hislop and Nagle, Northern Trading Co./Northern
Traders Ltd., Lamson and Hubbard, Canalaska Trading Co., Revillon Frères Ltd., independently
operated traders, native-owned posts, government stores, cooperative stores, mission posts), which
is the actual post-by-post register the researcher will want alongside the six regional Table 4–9
listings in Part One.

Notes:
- pdf pages 1–6 are title page, imprint, dedication, abstract, foreword and acknowledgements —
  no illustrations.
- No photographs were found anywhere in this volume — only maps, charts, and tables, matching
  the book's stated scope (a statistical/gazetteer report, not an illustrated narrative).
- Figures 1, 7, 8, 9 could not be read directly from OCR (they are graphic-only maps with little
  or no caption text extracted); their pdf-page placement is inferred from the authoritative
  front-matter List of Figures page numbers bracketed against the printed folios on neighbouring
  pages, which matched exactly for every figure that *could* be checked directly (2, 3, 4, 5, 6,
  11) — so confidence in the inferred positions is high, but a visual check is recommended before
  cutting.
- The Part Two ownership tables (pdf 80–92) repeat the same four-column format (Post Code /
  Location / Dates of Operation / Status) under a new company heading every page or two; this
  index groups them by section rather than listing every page individually — flip through pdf
  77–92 for the full run.

Total for B: 11 figures (maps/charts) + 10 numbered tables + a ~13-page continuous gazetteer of
posts by owner (Part Two) + Appendix A + 2 TOC-type front-matter pages.
