## The journal-*Arctic* licence rule, as applied here

Articles in *Arctic* carry a CC BY 4.0 licence only from about November 2016.
The Schledermann (1978, 1980), Helmer (1991) and Lynnerup (2003) articles this
topic works from carry none, so their artifacts and source rows say **rights
reserved, free to read at pubs.aina.ucalgary.ca**, and none of their figures is
reproduced. Request #50, still open, asks a person to fetch four post-2016 CC BY
*Arctic* PDFs whose figures could be registered under that licence.

## Positions that are not gazetteer positions

Checked against the Canadian Geographical Names Database on 2026-09-09: Port
Refuge (OANUX), Button Point (OACXT), Knud Peninsula (OAJFC), Native Point
(OALKF), Qaummaarviit (OBCLF) and Skraeling Island (OAPDT) all resolve.
**Alarnerk/Alaniq, Nunguvik and Uglit have no CGNDB entry**; their positions are
the excavators' published figures and are flagged as approximate on the pages
and in the rows. **Brooman Point carries a live disagreement**: CGNDB record
OACSJ is for the point, at 75.383333 N 97.291667 W, and there is no CGNDB record
for the village; Wikipedia's article places the village at 75.767 N 97.783 W
with no source, about 45 km to the north-northwest. The CGNDB figure is used and
the disagreement is stated, not smoothed.

## Claims deliberately not made

- The Qaummaarviit figures of "3,000 tools and 20,000 animal bones" and the
  gloss "the place that shines" are **not** in the Nunavut Parks brochure and no
  source for them has been traced; they were removed from the page, the artifact
  and the place row on 2026-09-09.
- The mobile-settlement quotation on p. 315 of Helmer 1991 is Helmer reporting
  Allen McCartney's 1989 argument, not his own.
- Rasmussen prints the "Before the present Iglulingmiut..." paragraph without
  naming a teller; only the Uglit passages that follow are signed *Told by
  Ivaluardjuk*.
