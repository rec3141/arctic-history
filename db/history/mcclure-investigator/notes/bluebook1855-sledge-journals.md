# Sledge journals of 1853 from Dealy Island — extraction notes

Source: `db/history/mcclure-investigator/files/mcclure-investigator-bluebook1855.txt`,
OCR of *Further Papers relative to the Recent Arctic Expeditions* (London, January
1855), 116,782 lines. All line numbers below are OCR line numbers in that text file
(`grep -n`); page numbers are the printed folio page nearest each cited line, taken
from a map of `(   NNN   )` page-marker lines built for this note (occasional pages
are missed by OCR, so some page numbers are the last confirmed marker before the
line, not necessarily the exact page of that word).

**Method and honesty notes (read before using the tables):**

- **[text]** = the coordinate, date, or quotation is printed in the OCR at the cited
  line. Sexagesimal values have been converted to decimal degrees (west negative);
  the conversion arithmetic is mine, the underlying degrees/minutes/seconds are the
  OCR's.
- **[inferred]** = no coordinate is printed for that entry; I have supplied an
  approximate modern coordinate for the *named place* from general historical-
  geography knowledge of the Canadian Arctic Archipelago (no web lookup was used —
  none was permitted). These are rounded and approximate, and are flagged with a
  confidence note.
- **coordinate omitted** = the place is named and dated in the text, but I do not
  have confident enough independent knowledge of that specific minor point/bay/cape
  to give a modern coordinate without guessing. Rather than invent a number, the
  cell is left blank and marked so. The date, place, and citation are still given.
- The OCR is a double-column folio with running side-notes that bleed into the main
  text and frequently scrambles digits (5⇄i, 8⇄S/3, 0⇄O, etc.). Where a printed
  number looks internally inconsistent I say so rather than silently "correcting" it.
- I never supplied a coordinate, date, page, or line number that isn't traceable to
  a specific grep/sed result recorded during this extraction.

## 1. Commander F. L. M'Clintock, sledge "Star of the North", 4 April–18 July 1853

Section header and orders: lines 66240–66420 (pp. 541–542). Confirmed dates "between
4th April and 18th July 1853" at line 66244. Day-by-day journal runs from line
66538 (4 April) to line 74454 (signature, "Commander H.M.S. Intrepid"), followed by
an abstract and a garbled "Table of Astronomical Observations" (lines 74495–74700+,
pp. 585–586). **Note on "Point M'Clintock":** I searched the whole file for
"Point M'Clintock", "M'Clintock's Point", "furthest west", "extreme west" and
"westernmost" (see log) and found no such name. "Cape M'Clintock" does occur several
times (lines 3458, 3465, 45091, 45713, 45726, 101492) but every instance is in an
unrelated narrative about Port Leopold / Cape Clarence on North Somerset Island —
a different cape, a different journey, not Prince Patrick Island. I have **not**
found a place called "Point M'Clintock" tied to this journey in this volume. His
farthest point west/north, so far as the printed journal shows, is among a group of
small islands off the west side of Prince Patrick's Land, reached about 13–17 June
1853 (see rows below); I identify that as his probable "furthest" rather than
asserting a name the text does not use.

printed date rather than silently matching the brief.

(checked lines 78500–82500); the rendezvous is described only by the coordinates

## Summary of what was and wasn't in the volume

- M'Clintock's "Star of the North" spring journey (4 Apr–18 Jul 1853): **printed in
  full**, lines 66240–74700ish.
- Mecham's "Discovery" spring journey: **printed in full**, but the volume's own
  dates say 4 April–**6 July** 1853, not 18 July as I was told to expect — I used
  the printed dates.
- Hamilton's "Hope" journey: **printed in full**, 27 April–20/21 June 1853. The
  Hamilton/Richards meeting is dated **18 May**, not 17 May, in this printed text.
  "Bathurst Island" is not named anywhere in Hamilton's journal.
- Pim: **both** his autumn-1852 Cape Providence depot journey (lines 77992–78581)
  **and** his spring-1853 Dealy-Island-to-Bay-of-Mercy journey (lines 82419–84450)
  are printed in this volume. I tabled the spring one, as the task asked, day by
  day.
- "Point M'Clintock" as a name: **not found** anywhere in this OCR text, despite a
  full-file search; I flagged this rather than inventing a location for it.
