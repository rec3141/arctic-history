=== relocations-qtc-055 place 1953-09
T: The old Inuit village on the west shore of Resolute Bay, 1953-1975
CR:  | BIB: qtc2013resolutebay | PG: pp. 24-25, 41-43
CREDIT: Qikiqtani Truth Commission, Community Histories 1950-1975: Resolute Bay (Qikiqtani Inuit Association, 2013)
LIC: this layer
FILE:  | DL: 
PEOPLE: []
D: Where the relocatees actually lived for twenty-two years. No site had been chosen before the ship came; the only instruction was that the settlement must not be too close to RCAF property, and in the end the detachment, the store and the houses went up about eight kilometres from the base and weather station, on the beach on the west side of Resolute Bay. Tents in the first autumn; then a hall built from timber off the weather-station dump, used for drying skins, as a workshop, as a church and, through the first dark season, as a classroom; then eleven scrap-built houses along the beach by 1957, each with one electric light run from the base; fourteen by 1960; eighteen by 1964. In the summer of 1975 every useful building was hauled about three kilometres to the present townsite on the east shore. The old site was left with a few unusable buildings and the cemetery. POSITION APPROXIMATE, to about 1.5 km: it is reckoned from the report's two measurements, 8 km from the base and 3 km west of the present townsite, which do not quite agree.
=== relocations-qtc-056 place 1955
T: Kuvinajuq: the ilagiit nunagivaktangat on Somerset Island, 1955-1960
CR:  | BIB: qtc2013resolutebay | PG: pp. 30-31
CREDIT: Qikiqtani Truth Commission, Community Histories 1950-1975: Resolute Bay (Qikiqtani Inuit Association, 2013)
LIC: this layer
FILE:  | DL: 
PEOPLE: []
D: The one camp away from the settlement that the Resolute Bay community history names. Three or four families lived at Kuvinajuq on Somerset Island between 1955 and 1960 rather than on the beach at Resolute Bay: the island had long been caribou country and Kuvinajuq a favoured site. It is roughly 300 km south of Resolute, and reaching it meant crossing Barrow Strait. The camp is not recorded as being closed by anyone; it simply stops appearing after 1960, as the houses, the wage work at the base and, from November 1960, the co-op drew people in. POSITION APPROXIMATE: the report places it only 'on Somerset Island at Bellot Strait and Creswell Bay', two places 90 km apart, and these coordinates take the Bellot Strait end.
